About Us

Our Story

Built on Proven Results

Long before The Bridge Foundation had a name, the work was already being done. Dave Fisher spent years in the field as a private trainer and coach, personally guiding more than 100 student athletes through the college recruiting process and athletic development programs through organizations like Steer for Student Athletes and Elite Squad Baseball. The results were clear. So was the need to scale.

The Foundation was built by the same coaches, advocates, and community leaders who had already been doing this work side by side. They had seen what happened when the right support reached the right athlete. That group now serves as the Foundation's Board of Directors, and their commitment hasn't changed, only the scale has.

The Bridge Foundation was created to take that impact further, reaching more athletes, across more sports, from more communities. The door that was opened for so many is now open wider than ever.

Our Services

What We Provide

Every resource an athlete needs to earn their spot at the next level.

Membership in the Foundation is 100% free. We provide financial assistance based on each athlete's individual need, because we believe financial constraints should never stand between talent and opportunity.

01

Financial Assistance

Membership in The Bridge Foundation is 100% free. Beyond that, we provide individualized financial assistance to cover the real costs of competing: training fees, showcase registrations, travel, equipment, and more. No athlete should be priced out of the process.

02

College Recruiting & Counseling

We guide athletes through every stage of the recruiting process: identifying the right programs, building a compelling profile, making direct contact with college coaches, and understanding what it takes to get an offer. We also assist with college applications, financial aid, and FAFSA navigation so athletes and families are never left figuring it out alone.

03

Academic Support

Eligibility is non-negotiable. We provide access to tutoring, study skills coaching, ESL support, and standardized test preparation. Our academic resources are built to keep athletes on track in the classroom so nothing gets in the way of their opportunities on the field.

04

Athletic Development

We invest in the athlete, not just the applicant. Training programs focus on strength, agility, and sport-specific skill development. We also provide funding for travel team participation, showcases, and exposure events where college coaches are watching. Getting seen is part of getting recruited.

05

Alumni Support

Our commitment does not end at enrollment. We provide scholarship support and ongoing guidance throughout an athlete's college career and into post-graduate life. Our athletes have a solid support system behind them at every turn, a dedicated team in their corner no matter where the road leads after signing day.

Everything It Takes to Get Recruited

College coaches are not looking for projects. They are looking for athletes who are ready: academically eligible, athletically developed, and visible in the right places. Every service we provide is built around making sure our athletes check every box. We remove the financial barriers. We develop the skill set. We handle the academic side. And we make sure each athlete is standing in front of the right coaches at the right time.

The results speak for themselves. Athletes placed at the college level across multiple sports and programs. A track record of success built on real relationships, real development, and real outcomes.

Our Impact

Overcoming the Odds

The Reality

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

For too many student athletes, the recruiting process rewards access over ability. Private coaches, travel teams, showcases, and exposure events all cost money that families in underserved communities simply do not have. The Bridge Foundation exists to change that.

The Barriers Are Real

Quality training, recruiting exposure, and academic preparation all carry a cost that locks out talented athletes from underserved communities. The result is real ability going unseen — not because these athletes aren't good enough, but because the system was never built to find them.

Behind every statistic is a young person whose potential was defined by circumstance, not capability. The Bridge Foundation was built to make sure more of them get the chance they deserve.

Support the Mission
70–75% of Division I baseball rosters are filled by players from affluent backgrounds
$5,000+ average annual cost for travel baseball, showcases, and recruiting exposure
2% of high school athletes earn a Division I scholarship

Proof That It Can Be Different

Behind every statistic is a real athlete with real potential. Our results are the direct answer to those numbers. Every athlete we place in college proves that when the right support is in place, the outcome changes. We have done it more than 80 times. We are not done.

Where an athlete comes from does not have to determine where they go. With your support, we keep proving that.

Sponsor an Athlete

Fund one student athlete's journey: the registrations, equipment, travel, and academic support that carry them from high school fields to college campuses. We pair you. Families keep their privacy.

How It Works

01

Choose Your Level

Pick a monthly sponsorship level. Groups and businesses can pool one sponsorship together.

02

We Pair You

BFFSA matches you with one current athlete and sends a first name introduction: their sport, their goals, and what their journey costs.

03

Follow the Journey

Progress updates through the season and a recap at year's end, all shared through the foundation with family approval.

Sponsorship Levels

Every level pairs you with one athlete. Higher levels fund more of the journey. Sponsorships run in 12 month terms.

Covers a season of registration and league fees.

Become a Sponsor

Outfits an athlete for the year and funds academic support like test prep.

Become a Sponsor

Sends an athlete to multiple showcases and campus visits each year.

Become a Sponsor

Cornerstone

$250/month

Funds nearly all of one athlete's annual journey, from first practice to campus visit.

Become a Sponsor

Prefer a one-time gift? Donate any amount and it supports the whole program.

Built on Trust

•

Pairing is done by the foundation. There is never a catalog of athletes to browse.

•

Families choose their privacy level, from first name and photo to fully anonymous.

•

All updates and communication flow through BFFSA, protecting everyone involved.

•

Funding never depends on performance. If a sponsor steps away, the athlete never feels it.

Common Questions

Can I sponsor an athlete I already know?
You can name an athlete as a preference when you sign up. We will privately confirm the family is comfortable, and the foundation retains discretion over all funds, which is what keeps your gift tax deductible. If that pairing is not the right fit, we place you where the need is greatest.
Will I meet my sponsored athlete?
Sponsorship is designed around privacy: updates come through the foundation, and there is no direct contact. Sponsors are always welcome at public BFFSA events. If you want to work with athletes directly, ask us about our volunteer mentor track, which has its own application and screening process.
What happens if I need to cancel?
You can cancel anytime. Your athlete's support continues without interruption, absorbed by the program fund, so no student ever feels a sponsor's circumstances change.
How do I know it's making a difference?
You receive progress updates on your paired athlete through the year, and every sponsor gets the annual impact report showing exactly where program funds went: registrations, equipment, travel, and academics.
Is my sponsorship tax deductible?
Yes. The Bridge Foundation for Student Athletes is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and every gift is tax deductible to the extent allowed by law. You will receive a receipt for each contribution. Many employers also match charitable gifts.
Can my business or group sponsor together?
Yes. A business, church group, or team of friends can pool any level and share one sponsorship under a single point of contact.

Questions before you start? Email dave@bffsa.org. Happy to talk it through.
