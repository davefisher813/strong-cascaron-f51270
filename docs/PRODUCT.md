# PRODUCT

## What it is

The public face of BFFSA: who we are, programs, impact, leadership, events, how to apply, how to donate, newsletter signup.

## Who it serves

Donors and sponsors, prospective student athletes and families, partners, board prospects, the community.

## Main flows

1. Learn: home, about, programs (Bridge Invitational, Bridge Rising Leaders Scholarship, Soccer ID, the Signing Class), athletes, leadership bios, news, impact numbers. Bridge Pathways, Summer Cookout, and Basketball ID are not named on the current page (unverified whether that is intended).
2. Apply: student application form to Airtable.
3. Register: event registration to Airtable.
4. Donate: fixed Stripe Payment Link on every page. Sponsor: Bridge Invitational tiers (single, foursome, bronze, silver, gold, platinum) through the checkout function with promo codes enabled.
5. Subscribe: mailing list to Airtable; Mailchimp sends.

## Impact framing (approved)

Two periods: before official launch (2019 to about 2024) recruiting assistance and placement for more than 50 student athletes; since launch (about two years) over 30 more placed plus the full wraparound program. Flyer stats (Aug 2026): over 40 athletes placed in the past two years, 3.3 organization-wide GPA, over $750K in university aid, 100% placement in that two-year window. Confirm with Dave before publishing new numbers.

## Intended experience

Warm, credible, fast on a phone. Every page leads with what BFFSA does for the reader.
